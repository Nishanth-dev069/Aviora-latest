            <h2 id="introduction">
                Introduction
            </h2>

            <p>
                Becoming a commercial pilot is a dream for many
                aviation enthusiasts. A career in the cockpit offers
                the opportunity to work in a highly professional,
                technically advanced and globally connected industry.
            </p>

            <p>
                For students in India, becoming a commercial pilot
                generally involves meeting the required eligibility
                criteria, completing aviation medical examinations,
                passing the required examinations and completing
                flight training to obtain a Commercial Pilot Licence
                (CPL).
            </p>

            <div class="highlight">

                <strong>Important:</strong>

                Pilot licensing requirements can change.
                Always verify the latest requirements with the
                Directorate General of Civil Aviation (DGCA)
                before beginning your training.

            </div>


            <h2 id="eligibility">
                Eligibility Requirements
            </h2>

            <p>
                Students interested in pursuing commercial pilot
                training should first check whether they meet the
                applicable eligibility requirements.
            </p>

            <h3>Educational Qualification</h3>

            <p>
                Students generally need to have completed the
                required level of schooling with the applicable
                subjects, particularly Physics and Mathematics,
                according to current DGCA requirements.
            </p>

            <h3>Age Requirement</h3>

            <p>
                Applicants must meet the minimum age requirements
                applicable to the licence they are applying for.
            </p>

            <h3>Medical Fitness</h3>

            <p>
                Aviation medical fitness is an important part of
                becoming a professional pilot. Candidates should
                complete the appropriate aviation medical assessment
                before making a major financial commitment to training.
            </p>


            <h2 id="dgca">
                DGCA Requirements
            </h2>

            <p>
                The Directorate General of Civil Aviation regulates
                civil aviation and pilot licensing in India.
                Aspiring pilots should understand the applicable
                licensing requirements before beginning their training.
            </p>

            <ul>

                <li>
                    Complete the required aviation medical examination.
                </li>

                <li>
                    Register for applicable DGCA examinations.
                </li>

                <li>
                    Complete the required theoretical subjects.
                </li>

                <li>
                    Complete the required flight training.
                </li>

                <li>
                    Maintain the required documentation and logbooks.
                </li>

            </ul>


            <h2 id="training">
                Commercial Pilot Training
            </h2>

            <p>
                Commercial pilot training combines classroom
                instruction with practical flight training.
                Students gradually develop the knowledge and
                skills required to operate an aircraft safely.
            </p>

            <h3>Ground School</h3>

            <p>
                Ground school covers the theoretical foundation
                required for professional aviation.
                Subjects may include aviation regulations,
                navigation, meteorology, aircraft systems,
                technical knowledge and other applicable areas.
            </p>

            <h3>Flight Training</h3>

            <p>
                Flight training provides students with practical
                experience under the supervision of qualified
                flight instructors.
            </p>

            <p>
                Students progressively learn aircraft handling,
                take-offs, landings, navigation, emergency
                procedures and other flight skills.
            </p>


            <h2 id="cost">
                How Much Does Pilot Training Cost in India?
            </h2>

            <p>
                The total cost of commercial pilot training can
                vary significantly depending on the flight school,
                aircraft type, training location, flying hours,
                accommodation and other expenses.
            </p>

            <p>
                Students should therefore request a detailed
                fee structure from the aviation academy and
                understand what is included before enrolling.
            </p>

            <h3>Typical Expenses May Include</h3>

            <ul>

                <li>Ground school fees</li>

                <li>Flight training fees</li>

                <li>Medical examination expenses</li>

                <li>DGCA examination-related fees</li>

                <li>Study materials</li>

                <li>Accommodation and living expenses</li>

                <li>Uniform and equipment</li>

            </ul>


            <h2 id="career">
                Career Opportunities After CPL
            </h2>

            <p>
                A Commercial Pilot Licence can open the door to
                several aviation career pathways, depending on
                the candidate's qualifications, experience and
                applicable regulatory requirements.
            </p>

            <h3>Possible Career Paths</h3>

            <ul>

                <li>Airline First Officer</li>

                <li>Flight Instructor</li>

                <li>Charter Pilot</li>

                <li>Corporate Aviation Pilot</li>

                <li>General Aviation Pilot</li>

            </ul>

            <p>
                Many aspiring airline pilots continue building
                their experience after obtaining their CPL and
                work toward meeting the requirements for airline
                operations and future career progression.
            </p>

            </p>
