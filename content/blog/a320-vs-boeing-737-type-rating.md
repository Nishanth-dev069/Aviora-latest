Choosing between an A320 and a Boeing 737 [Type Rating](https://avioraaviation.in/blog/what-is-type-rating-commercial-pilots) depends almost entirely on which airline or airline group you are targeting, since the two certifications are not interchangeable and each qualifies a pilot to fly a distinct aircraft family. Neither aircraft is objectively better to train on; the right choice is the one that matches your intended employer.

## &#x20;

## Why This Choice Matters

 

A Type Rating is aircraft specific, so selecting one over the other effectively narrows which airlines you become immediately eligible to fly for. Since Type Rating is a genuine financial and time investment, it is worth deciding based on realistic target employers rather than general preference alone.

## &#x20;

## Airbus A320 Type Rating Overview

 

The Airbus A320 family uses a fly by wire control system operated through a sidestick rather than a traditional yoke, along with flight envelope protections that assist pilots in avoiding certain unsafe flight conditions. This aircraft family is widely used across full service and low cost carriers in India, and its Type Rating tends to be one of the most commonly pursued among new commercial pilots in the country.

## &#x20;

## Boeing 737 Type Rating Overview

 

The Boeing 737 uses a more traditional yoke based control system with a different underlying automation philosophy compared to the A320. Pilots who train on the 737 often describe it as feeling closer to conventional aircraft handling, and it remains a widely used aircraft type among carriers operating Boeing fleets both in India and internationally.

 

## Key Differences in Training and Handling

 

The core difference between the two lies in cockpit philosophy rather than raw difficulty. The A320's sidestick and fly by wire protections are unfamiliar to pilots coming purely from small trainer aircraft, while the 737's yoke feels more directly connected to traditional flight training. Neither is inherently easier to learn, and most flight instructors agree that a well prepared candidate can adapt successfully to either system with proper training.

 

## How to Decide Which Type Rating to Pursue

 

The most reliable approach is to work backward from your target employer. If you are aiming for airlines that operate large A320 family fleets, pursuing an A320 Type Rating directly aligns your qualification with your intended job market. If your target airlines operate Boeing aircraft instead, a 737 Type Rating is the more strategic choice. For candidates without a firm airline preference yet, researching which aircraft type dominates the routes and airlines you are most interested in is a useful starting point before committing to either path.

 

## Cost and Duration Are Broadly Similar

 

Both A320 and Boeing 737 Type Ratings typically involve six to eight weeks of intensive training combining ground theory with simulator sessions, and both generally cost between fifteen and twenty five lakh rupees in India. Cost and duration are rarely the deciding factor between the two; employer alignment almost always matters more.

 

## Frequently Asked Questions

 

### Can I switch from an A320 Type Rating to a Boeing 737 Type Rating later in my career?

 

Yes, though this requires completing a separate Type Rating course for the new aircraft type, at additional cost and training time, and pilots do this throughout their careers when moving between airlines operating different fleets.

 

### Which Type Rating is easier for a beginner to learn?

 

Neither is meaningfully easier from a training difficulty standpoint. The two involve different cockpit philosophies rather than different levels of difficulty, and well prepared candidates succeed on either type with proper instruction.

 

### Does one Type Rating lead to better long term career prospects than the other?

 

Career prospects depend far more on which specific airlines you target and their hiring patterns than on which aircraft type you are rated on, since both A320 and Boeing 737 operators offer substantial long term career paths in India.
