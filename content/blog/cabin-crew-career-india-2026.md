A career as an airline cabin crew member (flight attendant) is one of the most exciting, globally mobile, and financially rewarding career paths available to young professionals in India. Beyond the allure of worldwide travel and luxury layover hotels, cabin crew members are certified safety professionals responsible for passenger safety, emergency medical response, evacuation management, and premium hospitality at 35,000 feet.

With Indian airlines adding over 1,500 new commercial aircraft to their fleets, hiring demand for cabin crew is at an all-time peak. This comprehensive 2026 guide covers eligibility standards, domestic vs. international salary structures, interview assessment rounds, and career progression.

---

## 1. Eligibility Criteria for Cabin Crew in India

While specific requirements vary slightly between domestic and international airlines, standard industry criteria include:

| Requirement | Domestic Airlines (IndiGo, Air India, Akasa) | International Airlines (Emirates, Qatar, Etihad) |
|---|---|---|
| **Educational Qualification** | 10+2 (Higher Secondary) passed in any stream | 10+2 passed (Degree preferred) |
| **Age Limit** | 18 to 27 years (Fresher) | 21 to 30 years |
| **Minimum Height (Female)** | 155 cm (approx. 5 ft 1 in) | Minimum arm reach of 212 cm on tiptoes |
| **Minimum Height (Male)** | 170 cm (approx. 5 ft 7 in) | Minimum arm reach of 212 cm on tiptoes |
| **BMI / Weight** | Proportional to height (BMI 18–22 for female, 18–25 for male) | In proportion to height |
| **Visual Acuity** | 6/6 or 6/9 in each eye (Corrective lenses / contacts permitted) | 6/6 with or without contact lenses |
| **Language Skills** | Fluency in spoken English and Hindi | Impeccable English (additional foreign languages a plus) |
| **Tattoos & Scars** | No visible tattoos or permanent marks in airline uniform | Strict zero visible tattoo policy |

## 2. Cabin Crew Salary Structure in 2026

Cabin crew compensation is structured as a combination of **Base Salary + Flying Hourly Pay + Layover / Daily Allowances (Per Diem) + Performance Incentives**.

### Domestic Airline Salary Scale (IndiGo, Air India, Akasa Air, SpiceJet):
- **Trainee / Probationary Cabin Crew:** ₹35,000 – ₹45,000 / month (during 3-month initial training).
- **Cabin Crew (Fresher - 0 to 2 years):** ₹55,000 – ₹85,000 / month (approx. 70–80 flying hours).
- **Senior Cabin Crew / Lead Attendant:** ₹90,000 – ₹1,25,000 / month.
- **In-Flight Service Manager (IFSM) / Cabin Senior:** ₹1,35,000 – ₹1,75,000+ / month.

### International Carrier Salary Scale (Emirates, Qatar Airways, Etihad, Singapore Airlines):
- Tax-free base pay + flying hours + overseas layover allowances.
- **Starting Pay:** ₹1,80,000 – ₹2,50,000+ per month (with fully furnished luxury accommodation and free transport provided in Dubai/Doha).

## 3. The Airline Selection Process: Assessment Day Breakdown

Securing a coveted airline cabin crew position requires standing out during competitive airline assessment days. Selection typically involves four rigorous stages:

1. **Grooming & Height/Reach Screening:** Instant evaluation of posture, professional uniform standards, skin clarity, dental aesthetics, and arm-reach compliance.
2. **Group Discussion (GD) & Extempore:** Testing spontaneous communication, active listening, voice diction, and cooperative team behavior under pressure.
3. **Emergency Scenario Handling & Customer Care Test:** Problem-solving exercises evaluating patience, empathy, conflict de-escalation, and passenger safety prioritization.
4. **Final 1-on-1 Panel Interview:** Behavioral and situational competency interview examining cultural adaptability, motivation, and resilience.
