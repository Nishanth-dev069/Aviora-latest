A Commercial Pilot Licence, commonly shortened to CPL, is the qualification issued by the DGCA that legally allows a pilot to be paid to fly an aircraft. It sits above a Student Pilot Licence and a Private Pilot Licence in India's pilot licensing structure, and it is the minimum licence required to begin a professional flying career, whether with an airline, a charter operator, or a cargo carrier.

 

## What a CPL Actually Allows You to Do

 

Holding a CPL means you are legally certified to act as a paid pilot, including working as a first officer on a commercial aircraft once you also hold the relevant Type Rating. Without a CPL, a pilot can fly recreationally under a Private Pilot Licence, but cannot legally accept payment for flying services of any kind.

 

## How a CPL Differs From a Student or Private Pilot Licence

 

A Student Pilot Licence is the earliest stage, allowing supervised flight training from the age of sixteen. A Private Pilot Licence, available from age seventeen, allows a pilot to fly independently but strictly for personal, non commercial purposes. A CPL, available from age eighteen, is the first licence that permits paid commercial flying, and is the standard entry qualification for any professional pilot career in India.

 

## What CPL Training Actually Involves

 

CPL training combines two parallel components. Ground school covers five core subjects set by the DGCA: Air Navigation, Aviation Meteorology, Air Regulations, Technical General, and Technical Specific. Flight training requires logging a minimum of two hundred flying hours, including specific requirements for cross country flying, instrument time, and night flying. Both components are formally examined, and a candidate must clear the DGCA's written examinations along with a practical flying skill test before the licence is issued.

 

## How Long Does CPL Training Take?

 

Most students complete CPL training, from the start of ground classes to receiving their licence, within eighteen to twenty four months, assuming consistent study and no major delays in medical clearance or flight training slot availability.

 

## Is a CPL the Same as Being Airline Ready?

 

Not quite. A CPL confirms foundational commercial flying competence, but airlines operate specific aircraft types, such as the Airbus A320 or Boeing 737, and flying these commercially requires an additional [Type Rating](https://avioraaviation.in/blog/what-is-type-rating-commercial-pilots) on top of the CPL. ## Frequently Asked Questions

 

### Can I get a CPL directly without a Private Pilot Licence first?

 

Most training pathways in India move through Student Pilot Licence and Private Pilot Licence stages as part of building toward the CPL, though the exact structure can vary slightly between training organisations.

 

### Does a CPL guarantee a job with an airline?

 

No. A CPL is the minimum qualification required to be considered for a commercial flying role, but hiring also depends on Type Rating, interview performance, and airline specific requirements.

 

### How much does CPL training cost in India?
