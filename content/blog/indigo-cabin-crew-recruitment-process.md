As India's largest airline carrying over 60% of domestic air traffic with a massive fleet of 350+ Airbus A320 and A321 aircraft (and an order book of 500+ more jets), **IndiGo (InterGlobe Aviation)** is the single largest employer of cabin crew in South Asia.

IndiGo conducts frequent walk-in recruitment drives and registered assessment days across major Indian metros (Hyderabad, Delhi, Mumbai, Bengaluru, Kolkata, Chennai, Pune, Chandigarh). However, with hundreds of applicants attending each drive, securing selection demands thorough preparation.

This guide delivers an insider's breakdown of every stage in the IndiGo cabin crew selection process, mandatory grooming benchmarks, group discussion themes, and top personal interview questions.

---

## 1. IndiGo Cabin Crew Eligibility Criteria

Before attending an IndiGo interview drive, ensure you meet the baseline criteria:

- **Gender:** Female candidates (for primary domestic narrowbody operations).
- **Nationality:** Indian Nationals holding a valid Indian Passport.
- **Age Limit:** 18 to 27 years (Freshers).
- **Minimum Height:** 155 cm (barefoot) with weight proportionate to height (BMI range: 18 – 22).
- **Education:** Passed 10+2 examination from a recognised Board or University.
- **Language Fluency:** Clear diction and fluency in both English and Hindi.
- **Appearance & Grooming:** Clear skin without visible tattoos, visible body piercings (other than one earlobe piercing), or prominent facial scars while wearing the half-sleeve IndiGo cabin crew tunic.

## 2. Step-by-Step Selection Stages on Assessment Day

IndiGo's assessment days are fast-paced elimination events comprising five distinct stages:

### Round 1: Document Verification & Physical Measurement
- Verification of 10th & 12th marksheets, Aadhaar card, and valid passport.
- Precision barefoot height measurement and BMI verification on medical scales.
- Initial skin, teeth alignment, and hands/nails grooming check by assessors.

### Round 2: Introduction & Grooming Screening (Fishbowl Round)
- Candidates enter in groups of 10–12 and stand in a semi-circle.
- You are given 30 to 45 seconds to introduce yourself: name, educational background, city, and why you wish to join IndiGo.
- **What Assessors Look For:** Warm smile, eye contact, upright posture, natural vocal tone, accent clarity, and professional makeup and hairstyle.

### Round 3: Group Activity / Group Discussion (GD)
- The group is given a situational prompt or general topic (e.g., *"Dealing with an irate business passenger when their preferred meal is unavailable"* or *"Safety vs. On-time departure"*).
- **What Assessors Look For:** Active listening, polite interjection, teamwork, empathy, and composure. Assessors immediately eliminate candidates who dominate aggressively or remain completely silent.

### Round 4: Personal Interview (PI) & Situational Test
The final round is a 1-on-1 or 2-on-1 interview with Senior Cabin Crew Base Managers and HR Assessors:
- **Common Behavioral Questions:**
  - *"Why do you want to work specifically for IndiGo rather than full-service carriers?"*
  - *"Describe a difficult customer service situation you resolved during college or past work."*
  - *"How will you handle working night shifts and missing family festivals?"*
  - *"What does '6E On-Time Performance' mean to you?"*

### Round 5: Medical Examination & Letter of Intent (LOI)
Shortlisted candidates receive an LOI followed by standard pre-employment medical checks at empaneled clinics (audiometry, vision, blood tests, spine X-ray, and drug screening).

## 3. How to Ace Your Interview: 4 Golden Rules

1. **Adopt the '6E' Spirit:** IndiGo values efficiency, punctuality, courteousness, and unpretentious service. Embody these traits in your body language and concise answers.
2. **Dress Flawlessly:** Wear a sharp Western formal business outfit (formal knee-length skirt or formal trousers with a pressed crisp shirt/blazer), clean neat hair bun with hairnet, classic nude/red lipstick, and well-maintained heels.
3. **Practice Live Emergency Scenarios:** Candidates who demonstrate clear knowledge of in-flight emergencies and passenger psychology perform significantly better.
4. **Train in a Live Mock Cabin:** Familiarity with aircraft galleys, jumpseats, and overhead bins builds unshakable confidence.
