Following successful capital infusion and fleet operationalisation, Indian budget carrier SpiceJet has announced the return of grounded Boeing 737 and Q400 aircraft to active service, accompanied by a nationwide recruitment drive for over **200 cabin crew positions** across its primary operating bases in Delhi, Mumbai, and Hyderabad.

### Selection Criteria for SpiceJet Cabin Crew

- **Eligibility:** 10+2 passed from a recognised educational board (any stream).
- **Age Limit:** 18 to 27 years (Freshers).
- **Physical Standards:** Minimum height 155 cm (Female) / 173 cm (Male) with weight proportionate to height.
- **Language & Personality:** Fluent spoken English and Hindi, pleasing personality, and clear skin with no visible tattoos in uniform.
