Dubai-based international carrier Emirates has announced dedicated assessment days across India—including recruitment drives in Hyderabad, Mumbai, Bengaluru, and New Delhi—seeking to hire more than 300 cabin crew members to join its multinational in-flight team.

### World-Class Career Benefits

Positions offer candidates an international lifestyle based in Dubai, flying across a global network of 140+ destinations on Airbus A380, Boeing 777, and new Airbus A350 aircraft.
- **Tax-Free Compensation:** Competitive starting pay averaging ₹1,80,000 to ₹2,50,000+ per month.
- **Full Accommodation & Transport:** Fully furnished private shared apartments in Dubai with complimentary company transport to and from Dubai International Airport (DXB).
- **Travel Privileges:** Concessional staff travel benefits across the global Emirates route network.

### What Emirates Recruiters Look For

Emirates assessors evaluate:
- **Arm Reach Requirement:** Minimum reach of 212 cm on tiptoes.
- **Interpersonal & Language Skills:** Fluent, articulate English with high emotional intelligence and customer empathy.
- **Professional Presentation:** Zero visible tattoos in uniform, confident posture, and immaculate grooming standards.
