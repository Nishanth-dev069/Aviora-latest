Award-winning international carrier Qatar Airways has announced frequency increases across its 13 Indian gateway cities (including Hyderabad, Mumbai, Delhi, Bengaluru, and Chennai), connecting Indian travelers to its global network of over 170 destinations via Hamad International Airport (DOH) in Doha.

### Cabin Crew Recruitment Assessment Days

To support network expansion and the induction of new Boeing 787-9 and Airbus A350-1000 aircraft, Qatar Airways is holding dedicated Cabin Crew Assessment Days in major Indian cities.

Key benefits of joining Qatar Airways:
- **Tax-Free Compensation & Per Diem:** Average monthly earning of ₹1,90,000 – ₹2,60,000+ tax-free.
- **Doha Lifestyle:** Fully furnished shared 5-star standard apartment in Doha, complete with swimming pool, gym, and 24/7 security.
- **Comprehensive Medical & Travel Benefits:** Full worldwide health insurance coverage and worldwide staff discount travel tickets.
