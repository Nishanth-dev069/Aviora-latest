India's market-leading airline, IndiGo (InterGlobe Aviation), has announced an extensive recruitment program targeting **500 First Officers** across 2026 to support its rapid aircraft induction schedule and international network expansion.

### Hiring Requirements and Candidate Profile

IndiGo is screening applications from both typed First Officers (Airbus A320) and fresh Commercial Pilot Licence (CPL) holders meeting DGCA regulatory criteria:
- **Licence Requirements:** Valid DGCA Commercial Pilot Licence (CPL) with Multi-Engine and Instrument Ratings.
- **Medical:** Valid DGCA Class 1 Medical Assessment. Read our [Medical Clearance Guide](/blog/dgca-class-2-medical-guide).
- **Type Rating Preference:** Candidates holding an Airbus A320 Type Rating with Level D simulator certification are prioritized for immediate induction. Learn more about [A320 Type Rating Programs](/programs/type-rating).
- **Technical Knowledge:** High proficiency in DGCA theory, with strong scores in [Air Navigation](/blog/dgca-air-navigation-cpl-study-guide) and [Aviation Meteorology](/blog/dgca-aviation-meteorology-cpl-guide).
